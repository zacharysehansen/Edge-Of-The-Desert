Data Sources and Credits
========================

The following sources provided data used in this project:

1. USGS ScienceBase - Public Supply Water Use Reanalysis
   [https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/65f86970d34e97daac9ff4c8](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/65f86970d34e97daac9ff4c8)
   U.S. Geological Survey. Public supply water use reanalysis for the 2000-2020
   period by HUC12, month, and year for the conterminous United States.

2. USGS ScienceBase - Irrigation Water Use
   [https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/6488734cd34ef77fcafe347a](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/6488734cd34ef77fcafe347a)
   U.S. Geological Survey. HUC12-level irrigation withdrawal estimates for
   Arizona, 2000-2020.

3. NASA Earthdata LP DAAC - MODIS/Terra Vegetation Indices Monthly L3 Global 1km SIN Grid V061 (MOD13A3)
   [https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/es/data/catalog/lpcloud-mod13a3-061](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/es/data/catalog/lpcloud-mod13a3-061)
   NASA Land Processes Distributed Active Archive Center (LP DAAC).
   MODIS/Terra monthly vegetation indices used for NDVI in phase 1.

4. NASA GES DISC - MERRA-2 statM_2d_slv_Nx, Monthly Single-Level Diagnostics (M2SMNXSLV)
   [https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/merra-2-statm-2d-slv-nx-2dmonthlyaggregated-statisticssingle-levelassimilationsingle-level-969e2](https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/merra-2-statm-2d-slv-nx-2dmonthlyaggregated-statisticssingle-levelassimilationsingle-level-969e2)
   NASA Goddard Earth Sciences Data and Information Services Center (GES DISC).
   MERRA-2 monthly single-level diagnostics used for 2m temperature.

5. NASA GES DISC - MERRA-2 tavgM_2d_flx_Nx, Monthly Surface Flux Diagnostics (M2TMNXFLX)
   [https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/merra-2-tavgm-2d-flx-nx-2dmonthly-meantime-averagedsingle-levelassimilationsurface-flux-di-45304](https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/merra-2-tavgm-2d-flx-nx-2dmonthly-meantime-averagedsingle-levelassimilationsurface-flux-di-45304)
   NASA Goddard Earth Sciences Data and Information Services Center (GES DISC).
   MERRA-2 monthly surface flux diagnostics used for precipitation.

6. NASA PO.DAAC / JPL - JPL TELLUS GRACE Level-3 Monthly Land Water-Equivalent-Thickness Surface Mass Anomaly Release 6.0 version 04
   [https://podaac-www.jpl.nasa.gov/dataset/TELLUS_GRAC_L3_JPL_RL06_LND_v04](https://podaac-www.jpl.nasa.gov/dataset/TELLUS_GRAC_L3_JPL_RL06_LND_v04)
   NASA Physical Oceanography Distributed Active Archive Center (PO.DAAC), Jet Propulsion Laboratory.
   GRACE monthly terrestrial water storage anomaly product used for the earlier part of the groundwater anomaly series.

7. NASA PO.DAAC / JPL - JPL TELLUS GRACE-FO Level-3 Monthly Land Water-Equivalent-Thickness Surface Mass Anomaly Release 6.0 version 04
   [https://podaac-www.jpl.nasa.gov/dataset/TELLUS_GRFO_L3_JPL_RL06_LND_v04](https://podaac-www.jpl.nasa.gov/dataset/TELLUS_GRFO_L3_JPL_RL06_LND_v04)
   NASA Physical Oceanography Distributed Active Archive Center (PO.DAAC), Jet Propulsion Laboratory.
   GRACE-FO monthly terrestrial water storage anomaly product used for the later part of the groundwater anomaly series.

8. U.S. Census Bureau - Population Estimates Program (PEP)
   [https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/popest.html](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/popest.html)
   U.S. Census Bureau.
   Annual county-level population estimates (intercensal 2000-2010, postcensal 2010-2023)
   for eight southern Arizona counties. Interpolated to monthly via cubic spline.

9. USGS NLCD - Annual Fractional Impervious Surface
   [https://www.mrlc.gov/data](https://www.mrlc.gov/data)
   Multi-Resolution Land Characteristics Consortium (MRLC).
   Annual fractional impervious surface rasters (30m resolution) for CONUS, clipped to
   the eight-county study area. Used to derive urbanization trend (impervious_pct).

10. U.S. Drought Monitor - DSCI Statistics API
   [https://droughtmonitor.unl.edu/](https://droughtmonitor.unl.edu/)
   National Drought Mitigation Center, University of Nebraska-Lincoln.
   Weekly county-level Drought Severity and Coverage Index (DSCI) for eight southern
   Arizona counties. Area-weighted and aggregated to monthly.

11. U.S. Bureau of Reclamation - Lake Mead Reservoir Data
   [https://www.usbr.gov/uc/water/hydrodata/reservoir_data/921/dashboard.html#csv/](https://www.usbr.gov/uc/water/hydrodata/reservoir_data/921/dashboard.html#csv/)
   U.S. Bureau of Reclamation, HydroData Navigator.
   Daily pool elevation, storage, total release, and release volume for Lake Mead
   (reservoir ID 921). Aggregated to monthly means for the 2000-2023 period.

12. NIFC - InterAgency Fire Perimeter History
   [https://data-nifc.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/nifc::interagencyfireperimeterhistory-all-years-view/](https://data-nifc.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/nifc::interagencyfireperimeterhistory-all-years-view/)
   National Interagency Fire Center (NIFC).
   Historical wildfire perimeter records for all years, filtered to Arizona fires.
   Used to build monthly wildfire risk index (fire count + log-acres).

13. Arizona Wildfire Records
   State of Arizona / compiled wildfire event data.
   Annual fire records with acreage for the southern Arizona region.
   Used to build annual wildfire risk index.

14. USGS - North American Breeding Bird Survey (BBS)
   [https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/691cfb53d4be021d1d89b482](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/691cfb53d4be021d1d89b482)
   U.S. Geological Survey, Patuxent Wildlife Research Center.
   2025 Release - North American Breeding Bird Survey Dataset (1966-2024).
   Route-level bird counts for routes within the eight-county study area,
   aggregated to an annual wildlife abundance index.

15. USGS NWIS Daily Values Service - Surface Water
   [https://waterservices.usgs.gov/nwis/dv/](https://waterservices.usgs.gov/nwis/dv/)
   U.S. Geological Survey, National Water Information System.
   Daily mean discharge (parameter 00060) and gage height (parameter 00065) from
   stream gages in eight southern Arizona counties. Aggregated to monthly regional means.

16. USGS NWIS Daily Values Service - Groundwater Levels
   [https://waterservices.usgs.gov/nwis/dv/](https://waterservices.usgs.gov/nwis/dv/)
   U.S. Geological Survey, National Water Information System.
   Daily mean depth to water level (parameters 72019, 72008, 62610, 62611) from
   monitoring wells in eight southern Arizona counties. Aggregated to monthly regional means.

17. Central Arizona Project - Water Delivery Reports
   [https://www.cap-az.com/cap-system/water-operations/deliveries/](https://www.cap-az.com/cap-system/water-operations/deliveries/)
   Central Arizona Water Conservation District. Monthly deliveries by
   classification (1999-2018) and year-to-date deliveries by contract type
   (2018-present), acre-feet. Total monthly CAP deliveries used as a pumping
   proxy probe and to calibrate the Lake Mead shortage lever.

18. Bureau of Reclamation - Colorado River Accounting and Water Use Reports
   [https://www.usbr.gov/lc/region/g4000/wtracct.html](https://www.usbr.gov/lc/region/g4000/wtracct.html)
   U.S. Bureau of Reclamation, Lower Colorado Basin Region. Annual decree
   accounting reports (Arizona, California, Nevada); the Central Arizona
   Project diversion at Lake Havasu, monthly, used as a cross-check.

19. NASA GES DISC - GLDAS Noah Land Surface Model L4 monthly 0.25 x 0.25 degree V2.1 (GLDAS_NOAH025_M)
   [https://disc.gsfc.nasa.gov/datasets/GLDAS_NOAH025_M_2.1/summary](https://disc.gsfc.nasa.gov/datasets/GLDAS_NOAH025_M_2.1/summary)
   Beaudoing, H. and M. Rodell, NASA/GSFC/HSL (2020), GLDAS Noah Land Surface
   Model L4 monthly 0.25 x 0.25 degree V2.1, Greenbelt, Maryland, USA, Goddard
   Earth Sciences Data and Information Services Center (GES DISC),
   doi:10.5067/SXAVCZFAQLNO. Soil moisture, snow water equivalent, canopy
   storage, evapotranspiration and rainfall forcing over the study bounding box,
   used as a GRACE feature probe.
