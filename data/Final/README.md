# Final Data Files

This folder stores the current Arizona-focused final endpoint/source files for phase 1 and the usable source CSVs generated by `scripts/phase1.py collect`.

Current endpoint/source files in this folder:

- `snotel_swe.csv`
  Monthly Arizona SNOTEL snow-water-equivalent feature aggregated from the
  Baker Butte, Happy Jack, Mormon Mountain, and Hannagan Meadows station bundle.
  Columns: `year_month, snow_water_equivalent_in`
  Coverage: monthly from `2000-10` through `2018-07`

- `irrigation_huc12_monthly_az_2000_2020.csv`
  Arizona statewide monthly irrigation withdrawal feature derived from the USGS
  irrigation HUC12 source matrix.
  Columns: `year_month, irrigation_total_withdrawal_mgd, irrigation_total_withdrawal_gallons_per_day, irrigation_total_withdrawal_acre_feet_month, huc12_count`
  Coverage: monthly from `2000-01` through `2020-12`
  Note: this is the current aggregated irrigation endpoint written for phase 1.

- `nwaa_public_supply_az_monthly.csv`
  Arizona statewide monthly public-supply groundwater feature aggregated from
  NWAA HUC12 shards.
  Columns: `year_month, public_supply_groundwater_mgd, public_supply_groundwater_gallons_per_day, public_supply_groundwater_acre_feet_month, huc12_count`
  Coverage: monthly from `2000-01` through `2020-12`

- `powell_combined.csv`
  Lake Powell monthly operational feature bundle merged from manually gathered
  source files in `data/raw`.
  Columns: `year_month, powell_evaporation, powell_total_release, powell_inflow, powell_storage, powell_pool_elevation`
  Coverage: monthly from `2000-01` through `2020-12`

- `azpop_monthly.csv`
  Arizona monthly population feature expanded from annual `AZPOP` observations.
  Columns: `year_month, AZPOP`
  Coverage: monthly from `2000-01` through `2020-12`

- `modis_ndvi.csv`
  Arizona-wide monthly NDVI feature collected from MODIS Earthdata.
  Columns: `year_month, ndvi`
  Coverage: monthly from `2000-02` through `2023-12`

- `merra_precipitation.csv`
  Arizona-wide monthly MERRA-2 precipitation feature derived from the locally
  downloaded `data/raw/merra_precipitation/*.nc4` files.
  Columns: `year_month, precipitation_mm_day`
  Coverage: monthly from `2000-01` through `2023-12`

- `merra_temperature_2m.csv`
  Arizona-wide monthly MERRA-2 2m temperature feature derived from the locally
  downloaded `data/raw/merra_temperature_2m/*.nc4` files.
  Columns: `year_month, temperature_2m_c`
  Coverage: monthly from `2000-01` through `2023-12`

- `grace_groundwater_anomaly.csv`
  Arizona-wide monthly GRACE groundwater anomaly feature derived from the GRACE
  / GRACE-FO raw files in `data/raw/grace_groundwater_anomaly/`.
  Columns: `year_month, grace_groundwater_anomaly`
  Coverage: monthly from `2000-01` through `2020-12`

- `usgs_streamflow.csv`
  Monthly mean streamflow feature derived from the configured USGS NWIS daily
  gauge set.
  Columns: `year_month, streamflow_cfs`
  Coverage: monthly from `2000-01` through `2023-12`

- `usdm_sustainability.csv`
  Monthly Arizona drought feature built from U.S. Drought Monitor DSCI output,
  including the derived sustainability score used by the pipeline.
  Columns: `year_month, usdm_dsci, usdm_sustainability`
  Coverage: monthly from `2000-01` through `2023-12`
